Academic & Engineering Disclosure: Real Traffic vs Benchmark Metrics

The displayed 1.000 F1 score is evaluated against the 4,000-sample balanced baseline benchmark generated during training (see datasets/README.md). In real-world live networks, unseen packet patterns, encrypted TLS handshakes, and variable latency will introduce variance. Our hybrid defense strategy uses deterministic threshold rules as the primary ground truth and machine learning inference for supplementary scoring and anomaly triage.
